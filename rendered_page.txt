The Story

What started as helping friends and family with simple websites has evolved into a passion for creating digital experiences that truly make a difference. Based in Nigeria's capital, I've had the privilege of working with diverse businesses across various industries.

In 2022, I founded Alpha-Link Digital Solutions (ALDS) to bring together my technical skills with strategic thinking. The goal was simple: help businesses not just get online, but thrive online.

2025 marked a significant milestone with my completion of the ALX Software Foundation program. This program sharpened my problem-solving skills and introduced me to modern development methodologies, collaborative workflows, and industry best practices.

Currently pursuing my degree at NOUN, I believe in continuous learning and staying ahead of the rapidly evolving digital landscape.
